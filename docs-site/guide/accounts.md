# Accounts and groups

Select an account to choose a game, edit its profile, or open the Roblox website as that account.

## Edit a profile

Select an account, change its alias, groups, or notes, and select **Save Profile**. The app stores your notes and Roblox sign-ins in macOS Keychain.

To open the Roblox website for a saved account, use **Open Roblox Website** and choose Home, My Profile, Settings, or Security. The same choices are available when you right-click an account.

## Organize accounts into groups

1. Open the group menu and choose **New Group…**, or use **Groups > New Group…** on an account.
2. Add accounts to the group from their profile or right-click menu.
3. Choose the group from the filter to see its accounts. To launch them together, choose it under **Select Group**.

An account can belong to more than one group. Deleting a group removes it from accounts and Launch Sets; it does not delete the accounts.

## Check a sign-in

Select an account and choose **Check Account** in its profile. If the app shows **Sign In Again**, select it, sign in to that Roblox account, and choose **Save and Continue**. Your profile details, groups, notes, favorite games, and Launch Sets stay the same.

Most people can add an account with **Sign In on Roblox**. The advanced **Paste Session Value** option is for people who already have a `.ROBLOSECURITY` value. Anyone with that value can access the account, so do not share it.

## Remove an account

Close Roblox for that account if it is running. Then right-click the account, choose **Remove Account**, and confirm. Removing the account deletes its saved sign-in and profile note from this Mac. A normal backup does not contain either item, though it can preserve other account details. See [Backup and privacy](./backup).
